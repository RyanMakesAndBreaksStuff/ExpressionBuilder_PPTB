# tokens.css audit

Target: `packages/builder-ui/src/theme/tokens.css` (3,013 lines). Over-engineering only; split comes after these land.

Reviewed against `packages/` usage. A literal class-name search misses classes composed in TS (`eb-action-${variant}`). The cuts below are the ones that survived that check. The first-pass estimate of -560 lines counted the live Import/Export rules and a few shrinks that are not pure deletes.

> **REVIEW (2026-09-30):** every dead-selector and dead-token claim below re-verified by word-boundary scan over `packages/**/*.{ts,tsx,html}` excluding `dist/` — all 30 dead selectors and all 17 dead aliases confirmed, nothing missed in either direction. Three blockers and ~40 further lines are marked inline. Two proposals are regressions as written (see 480px and 700px merges), one breaks a test the doc does not mention, and one lookup helper is described incorrectly.
