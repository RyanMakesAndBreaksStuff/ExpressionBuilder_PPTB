How to use
----------
Each entry gives the file, the placeholder line, the scene name, and the
capture brief. Replace the blockquote in that file with an image reference in
the existing repo style:

  ![Alt text](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RyanMakesAndBreaksStuff/ExpressionBuilder_PPTB/main/assets/images/<file>.png)

Keep the alt text descriptive; it is what screen readers announce.
